## Specific Practice Texts — Location Guide

### Dark Retreat (mun mtshams)
- Found within the **nam mkha' mdzod** (Sky Treasury) cycle
- **Volumes 9–10** of MW1PD133161 / MW4CZ58530
- Full title: *yang zab nam mkha' mdzod chen las snyan rgyud* (oral transmission cycle)
- **Recommendation**: Use MW4CZ58530 (open access scans + OCR etext) or MW1PD133161 etext (excellent quality)

### A-khrid (A-tri Meditation System)
- Part of the **khrid rim** (graduated instruction) cycle
- Look in **Volume 2** (sngon 'gro'i khrid rim) and **Volume 16** (nyams khrid)
- A-khrid is Shardza's primary meditation system lineage

### Thögal (thod rgal / Leap-Over)
- Contained within the **rdzogs pa chen po sku gsum rang shar** (Volume 3)
- Also in **dbyings rig mdzod** volumes (11–12) which cover the path and fruit
- Thögal is the counterpart practice to trekchö in the Dzogchen system
