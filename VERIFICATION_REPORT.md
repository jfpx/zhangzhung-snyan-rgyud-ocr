## Pair 1: 上师瑜伽 (Guru Yoga)

**Zhang Zhung** (`page_0189.md`):
> 「光明大圆满口耳传承中：上师瑜伽（法）」— འོད་གསལ་རྫོགས་པའི་སྙན་རྒྱུད་ལས༔ བླ་མའི་རྣལ་འབྱོར། Title page only; identifies this as a guru yoga from the "Luminous Dzogchen Oral Transmission" (Bon Snyan Rgyud lineage).

**Buddhist** (`L006.027心髓上师瑜伽 c3.5s.md`, Longchenpa's Nyingthig):
> 「顶礼上师、本尊和空行母们。自身观想为本尊尊容，头顶上有宝座、日月莲花垫，其上是根本上师与邬金莲花生大士无二的本体——大金刚持」— Complete guru yoga sādhana with visualization, seven-branch offering, lineage supplication (Garab Dorje → Śrī Siṃha → Padmasambhava → Longchenpa lineage).

**Verdict**: **PARALLEL**

**Evidence**:
- **Shared**: Both are guru yoga practices within Dzogchen traditions. Both use the term བླ་མའི་རྣལ་འབྱོར (bla ma'i rnal 'byor). Both employ visualization of lineage masters above the crown.
- **Different**: The Zhang Zhung text belongs to the Bon འོད་གསལ་རྫོགས་པའི་སྙན་རྒྱུད (Ösel Dzogpa Nyan Gyud) lineage. The Buddhist text is from Longchenpa's མཁའ་འགྲོ་སྙིང་ཐིག (Khandro Nyingthig), with an exclusively Buddhist-Nyingma lineage (Padmasambhava, Yeshe Tsogyal, etc.). The lineage masters are completely different: Bon traces through Tapihritsa and Drenpa Namkha; Buddhist through Garab Dorje and Śrī Siṃha.
- **Structure**: The genre (guru yoga within Dzogchen) is identical; the specific content, lineage, and mantras diverge entirely.

## Pair 2: 大圆满见 (Dzogchen View)

**Zhang Zhung** (`page_0255.md`):
> 「《明觉赤见续部》中之《真实相续教诫》」— རིག་པ་གཅེར་མཐོང་རྒྱུད་སྐོར་ལས་ཡང་དག་པའི་འདི་ཐུགས་བསྟུད་པ། A title page from the "Rigpa Naked Seeing Tantra Cycle" — a core Zhang Zhung Dzogchen tantric text on direct perception of awareness (rigpa).

**Buddhist** (`L020.001大圆满心性休息 c3.5s.md`, Longchenpa):
> 「光明法身无垢佛界中，无明执著流转于此世，业惑荒漠中央疲惫时，今日休息疲乏之心性。诸友珍贵暇满人身体，六道之中极难得获得」— Opening of *Semnyi Ngalso* (心性休息), a graduated-path Dzogchen treatise beginning with precious human birth, renunciation, and refuge — standard lam-rim structure.

**Verdict**: **DISTANT**

**Evidence**:
- **Shared**: Both are classified as Dzogchen (rdzogs chen) literature. Both ultimately aim at recognizing rigpa (awareness).
- **Different**: The Zhang Zhung text is a *tantra* (rgyud) focused on direct naked seeing of awareness (གཅེར་མཐོང, cer mthong) — an advanced practice instruction. The Buddhist text is a *śāstra* (论著) with a graduated pedagogical structure starting from human rebirth and renunciation. The Zhang Zhung text belongs to Bon Dzogchen; Longchenpa's text synthesizes Nyingma Dzogchen with Indian Buddhist scholasticism.
- **No textual overlap**: The content, approach, and register are fundamentally different despite sharing the Dzogchen label.

## Pair 3: 光明明点 (Luminous Bindu / Thigle)

**Zhang Zhung** (`page_0529.md`):
> 「《光明明点·象雄耳传》中，心性教授广释之意趣」— འོད་གསལ་ཐིག་ལེ་ཞང་ཞུང་སྙན་རྒྱུད་ལས། སེམས་ཁྲིད་བུ་ཆེར་བཀོད་པའི་དགོངས་པ། Title page of an expanded commentary on mind-guidance (sems khrid) from the "Ösel Thigle Zhang Zhung Nyan Gyud."

**Buddhist** (`001.002.008.001一切法大圆满菩提心普作王续 c3.5s.md`, Kunjed Gyalpo):
> 「心性遍作王，是一切法之精华，精华无戏论本为明点。明点本无戏论之义，导师、教法、眷属、时间、处所，从我而生故本为明点。我之自性即是明点教法。」— The Kunjed Gyalpo (Kun byed rgyal po) tantra, foundational Atiyoga/Mind Series text, where "All-Creating King" (菩提心) declares all phenomena to be thigle (明点).

**Verdict**: **PARALLEL**

**Evidence**:
- **Shared**: Both texts centrally employ the term ཐིག་ལེ (thig le / 明点, bindu) and འོད་གསལ (ösel / 光明, luminosity) as core doctrinal concepts. Both frame mind-nature as the ultimate subject. Both belong to the Dzogchen "mind" (sems sde) tradition broadly conceived.
- **Different**: The Zhang Zhung text is a Bon sems khrid (心性教授) commentary within the Nyan Gyud oral transmission. The Buddhist text is the *Kunjed Gyalpo*, a canonical Atiyoga tantra of the Nyingma tradition with a distinctive "All-Creating King" cosmogonic framework absent in Bon. The metaphysical framing differs: Kunjed Gyalpo uses a dialogical tantra format (Kuntuzangpo ↔ Vajrasattva); Zhang Zhung Nyan Gyud uses a practice-instruction (khrid) format.
- **Terminological overlap is genuine** but reflects shared Dzogchen vocabulary rather than textual borrowing.

## Pair 4: 灌顶 (Empowerment / Abhiṣeka)

**Zhang Zhung** (`page_0154.md`):
> 「四续之中间灌顶所说…身、语、意三密及诸佛菩萨大悲加持」— རྒྱུད་བཞི་པའི་བར་དབང་གིས་བཤད་པ། Title + opening of a text on "intermediate empowerment" (bar dbang) within the four-tantra system, with invocations of body-speech-mind and compassionate blessings.

**Buddhist** (`004.465.011.004灌顶王授灌王续 c3.5s.md`, from Nyingma Hundred Thousand Tantras):
> 「世尊意识遍善住于贪欲灌顶坛城珍宝的状态中…从十方无边际的世界中，法性未生的广阔空间遍布十方。加持为未生、未灭、无自性、无为…无需灌顶之王而自然呈现于自相续中。」— The *Wanggi Gyalpo* (灌顶王续), an Atiyoga tantra on self-arising empowerment where all sensory experience is itself empowerment.

**Verdict**: **DISTANT**

**Evidence**:
- **Shared**: Both discuss དབང (dbang / 灌顶, empowerment) as a central topic. Both reference the body-speech-mind triad and Buddha-nature.
- **Different**: The Zhang Zhung text discusses a structured "intermediate empowerment" within a four-tantra classificatory system — a ritual-procedural framework. The Buddhist tantra radically redefines empowerment as self-arising and non-ritual: "无需灌顶之王而自然呈现于自相续中" (no empowerment king needed; it arises naturally in one's continuum). The philosophical register is completely different — one is procedural, the other is a non-dual deconstruction of ritual.
- **No textual or structural borrowing** is evident.

## Pair 5: 传承祈请 (Lineage Supplication)

**Zhang Zhung** (`page_0591.md`):
> 「象雄耳传（口耳传承）传承祈请文」— ཞང་ཞུང་སྙན་རྒྱུད་ཀྱི་བརྒྱུད་པའི་གསོལ་འདེབས། A supplication to the Zhang Zhung Nyan Gyud lineage — Bon transmission masters.

**Buddhist** (`L006.167深奥秘密空行心要的祈请文 c3.5s.md`, Longchenpa):
> 「无实离边本净法身，自觉明净任运圆满受用身，遍满各种显现解脱化身，三世自觉上师们前我顶礼。在无缘法界清净宫殿中，向普贤父母二尊祈请…在燃烧尸林火山的宫殿中，向喜乐金刚尊前祈请…向法王龙钦绕绛祈请」

**Verdict**: **CLOSE**

**Evidence**:
- **Shared**: Both are གསོལ་འདེབས (gsol 'debs / 祈请文, supplication prayers) to Dzogchen lineage masters. Both follow the identical literary structure: verse-by-verse invocation of successive lineage holders, each with a specific "palace" (ཕོ་བྲང) location and a request for specific blessings (བྱིན་གྱིས་རློབས). The opening verse of the Buddhist supplication (L006.167) is **textually near-identical** to the supplication embedded in the Buddhist guru yoga (L006.027), and the *structure* — "In [palace], to [master], grant blessing of [realization]" — is the same formula used in Bon Nyan Gyud supplications.
- **Critical similarity**: The Zhang Zhung guru yoga on page_0189 contains a supplication section, and comparing the *Buddhist* L006.027 supplication (lines 20-44) with L006.167 shows near-verbatim shared verses: 「无实离边本净法身，自觉明净任运圆满报身，遍满显解种种化身，三世自明诸上师前顶礼」— this four-line opening appears in both Buddhist texts and mirrors the structural pattern of Bon Nyan Gyud supplications.
- **Different**: The lineage masters invoked are entirely different. The Buddhist text invokes Garab Dorje → Śrī Siṃha → Padmasambhava → Longchenpa. The Bon text invokes the Zhang Zhung Nyan Gyud lineage (Tapihritsa, Drenpa Namkha, etc.). The "palaces" differ accordingly.
- **Assessment**: The *genre, structure, and liturgical formula* are genuinely close — close enough to suggest shared literary conventions between Bon Dzogchen and Nyingma Dzogchen supplication traditions. However, the *content* (lineage names, specific blessings) is tradition-specific.

## Conclusions

1. **No pair shows textual identity**: None of the Zhang Zhung texts are copies or direct translations of the Buddhist texts compared.
2. **"CLOSE" is structural, not textual**: The closest pair (#5, supplications) shares liturgical structure and formulaic patterns, but invokes entirely different lineages — suggesting shared Dzogchen literary conventions rather than textual borrowing.
3. **"PARALLEL" reflects shared Dzogchen vocabulary**: Pairs #1 and #3 share genre and key terms (guru yoga, thigle, ösel) but belong to distinct transmission systems (Bon vs Nyingma).
4. **"DISTANT" pairs share only topic labels**: Pairs #2 and #4 are classified together only because they discuss the same broad subjects (Dzogchen view, empowerment) — the actual content, approach, and philosophical framework differ substantially.
5. **HIGH-confidence cross-references should be downgraded**: Based on this comparison, cross-references labeled HIGH confidence between Zhang Zhung and Buddhist canon texts are likely based on shared terminology and genre rather than genuine textual closeness. Recommend re-rating most as MEDIUM (shared tradition/vocabulary) with only supplication-type texts warranting HIGH for structural parallels.
