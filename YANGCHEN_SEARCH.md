## Content & Structure

The book is described as the "传承篇" (Transmission/Lineage chapter) of the Zhang Zhung Snyan Rgyud, specifically covering the testaments (遗嘱/证悟) of the ancient masters of the Snyan Rgyud lineage.

### Table of Contents (reconstructed from Google AI Overview and FPYB)

Based on the Bön doctrinal structure of the Zhang Zhung Snyan Rgyud, the book has **approximately three major sections** plus supplementary materials:

1. **外篇 (Outer Section)**: 外在起源的因缘 (Origins/Lineage History)
   - Content: Covers the origins and visionary lineage history
   - Key chapter: Commentary on 《雍仲二十五》(interpretation of the "Twenty-Five Root Text"), also known as 《大成就者悟旨二十五颂经》

2. **内篇 (Inner Section)**: 证悟验教 (Experiential Teachings)
   - Content: Records of practitioners' experiences and realization
   - Key topics: Detailed explanation of the "七种明灯" (Seven Lamps) system of practice, including 基灯 (Base Lamp), 存在灯 (Existence Lamp), 道迹灯 (Path Lamp), 闲暇灯 (Leisure Lamp), 呈现灯 (Manifestation Lamp), 中阴灯 (Bardo Lamp)

3. **秘密篇 (Secret Section)**: 明噶赤磋亚要 (Essential Instructions)
   - Content: Relates to 秘密/essential practices, purification of the base and path
   - Key topics: Detailed explanation of secret teachings on practice within the Zhang Zhung Oral Transmission

4. **密秘篇 (Extremely Secret Section)**: 因缘本质 (Nature of Origins)  
   - Content: Most core, most profound section of the lineage teachings (二十一种铠甲 / 21 types of armor)
   - Key topics: The "二十一种铠甲" (21 Sacred Armors) — step-by-step guide for meditation and realization

Additionally, the text includes:
- **传承感悟谱系** (Lineage genealogy of realization)
- **45 幅古代大师擦擦 (Tsakali) drawings/paintings** reproduced in full color, including:
  - 9 位名义传承上师 (9 Named Lineage Masters)  
  - 35 位传承上师 (35 Transmission Masters)
  - 1 位总结性证悟大师 (1 concluding testament master)
- **传承上师证悟记录** (Records of masters' spiritual testaments/证悟)
- **24位大迫切成就者、辛饶弥沃大师、噶玛巴金刚大师的证悟和信仰心要** (testaments of 24 great urgency practitioners, Shenrab Miwoche, and Karma Vajra masters)

## About the Translator: 杨辰 (Chen Yang)

- Chinese Bönpo practitioner and qualified translator
- Began studying Yungdrung Bön in 2010
- Has frequently traveled between mainland China and Tibet to study the ancient culture
- Dedicated to the rescue, compilation, and translation of Bön literary heritage
- Welcomed as translator by FPYB (Foundation for the Preservation of Yungdrung Bön) in October 2019
- This translation was completed under the guidance of Yongdzin Lopön Tenzin Namdak Rinpoche
